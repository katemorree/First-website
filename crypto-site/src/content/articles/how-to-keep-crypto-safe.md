Most crypto losses aren't sophisticated hacks. They're phishing links, fake support agents, reused passwords and lost recovery phrases. These steps prevent the vast majority of them.

## Protect your exchange accounts

1. **Use a unique, long password** stored in a password manager.
2. **Turn on two-factor authentication** with an authenticator app or passkey — not SMS, which can be hijacked with a SIM swap.
3. **Set an anti-phishing code** so you can recognise real emails from the exchange.
4. **Enable withdrawal address whitelisting** so funds can only go to addresses you've approved.
5. **Bookmark the real website** and only log in from the bookmark.

## Protect your wallet

6. **Write your recovery phrase on paper or metal.** Never photograph it, type it or store it in the cloud.
7. **Store backups in two secure places**, such as a home safe and a trusted family member's home.
8. **Use a hardware wallet** for larger amounts. See our [hardware wallet guide](/learn/best-hardware-wallet).
9. **Buy hardware wallets only from the official store** — never second-hand.

## Avoid scams

10. **Nobody legitimate will ask for your recovery phrase.** Not support, not an exchange, not a wallet company.
11. **Ignore DMs offering help, giveaways or "guaranteed" returns.** "Send 1 ETH, get 2 back" is always a scam.
12. **Check every transaction before you sign it** — especially "approve" requests in DeFi apps.

## Common scams to recognise

| Scam | What it looks like |
| --- | --- |
| Fake support | Someone replies to your public complaint offering help, then asks for your phrase |
| Giveaway | Deepfake video of a celebrity promising to double your crypto |
| "Pig butchering" | A friendly stranger slowly convinces you to invest on a fake platform |
| Fake apps | Lookalike wallet apps in app stores or ads |
| Address poisoning | Tiny transactions from an address that looks like one you use |

If you've been scammed, report it to your local police and your country's fraud reporting service. Be wary of "recovery services" — most are scams too.
