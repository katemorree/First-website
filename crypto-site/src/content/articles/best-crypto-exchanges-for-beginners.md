For a beginner, the "best" exchange isn't the one with the most coins or the lowest futures fees. It's the one that makes it easy to buy, hard to make costly mistakes, and keeps your money safe.

## How we judged them

- **Ease of use** for someone buying their first crypto
- **Security track record** and transparency
- **Regulation** in major markets
- **Fees** for typical small purchases
- **Availability** in different countries

Scores are our own editorial opinion and are **not** influenced by affiliate commissions. See our [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure).

## Our picks

### Kraken — best overall for beginners

Strong security reputation since 2011, a simple app, and much lower fees if you switch to Kraken Pro once you're comfortable.

[[platform:kraken]]

### Coinbase — easiest first purchase

The most straightforward app. You pay for that simplicity with higher fees on simple buys.

[[platform:coinbase]]

### Binance — best for low fees (in supported countries)

The lowest base fees and widest selection. Use **Lite mode** to keep things simple. Not available in the US.

[[platform:binance]]

### Bybit — best app for future active traders

A polished, fast app. Stick to spot trading while you learn — derivatives are very prominent. Not available in the US.

[[platform:bybit]]
