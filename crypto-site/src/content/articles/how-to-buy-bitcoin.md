Buying Bitcoin is easier than it looks. The whole process takes five steps, and the most important one is choosing a trustworthy place to buy.

> Bitcoin's price is highly volatile. Only invest money you can afford to lose. This guide is educational, not financial advice.

## Step 1: Choose an exchange

An exchange lets you swap your local currency for Bitcoin. Look for:

- **Availability** in your country
- **A long track record** and clear regulation
- **Low fees** for the payment method you'll use
- **An easy app** if you're new

Compare options on our [exchanges page](/exchanges).

## Step 2: Create and verify your account

Reputable exchanges require identity verification (often called KYC — "Know Your Customer"). You'll usually need a photo ID and sometimes proof of address. This is a legal requirement and a good sign, not a red flag.

Immediately turn on **two-factor authentication** using an authenticator app.

## Step 3: Add money

Common options:

| Method | Speed | Typical cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bank transfer | Minutes to 1–2 days | Low or free |
| Debit card | Instant | Higher (often 1–4%) |
| Apple Pay / Google Pay | Instant | Similar to card |

Fees change often — always check the exchange's fee page before paying.

## Step 4: Buy Bitcoin

- **Simple "Buy" button:** easiest, but usually includes a spread (a hidden markup).
- **Advanced / Pro trading screen:** lower fees. Place a **limit order** to choose the exact price you pay.

Use our [trading fee calculator](/tools/trading-fee-calculator) to see what fees cost you on a given trade size.

## Step 5: Decide where to keep it

- **Leave it on the exchange:** convenient for small amounts, but the exchange holds your keys.
- **Move it to your own wallet:** you hold the keys. Best for larger or long-term holdings. See [how to withdraw crypto to a wallet](/learn/how-to-withdraw-crypto-to-a-wallet) and our [wallet comparison](/wallets).

## Watch out for

- Fake exchange apps in app stores — download from the official website link
- Anyone promising to "trade for you" or double your Bitcoin
- Bitcoin ATMs with very high fees
