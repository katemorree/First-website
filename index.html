Anatomy of the smash

The Press

Loose beef meets a hot flat-top and gets one hard press. No moulding, no shaping — that press is where everything starts.

The Crust

Seconds of full contact turn the edges lacy, brown and crisp. That caramelised crust is the whole reason to smash a burger.

The Build

Cheese goes on while it is still moving, then it is stacked and wrapped to travel — so a takeaway arrives the way it left.
